My Story

Building the Future,
One Line at a Time.

I'm Arpeet Patel, a first-year Computer Science student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur (Symbiosis International University).

My interest in technology isn't just academic — it's about solving real problems. Whether it's forecasting retail demand with ML, tracking charitable donations transparently on blockchain, or building responsive corporate websites during my internship, I approach every challenge with curiosity and a desire to create impact.

I hold certifications from Red Hat, Infosys Springboard, Deloitte Australia, and Hertie School, covering domains from Linux system administration to LLM engineering and agentic AI. I've also earned all three levels of the International Award for Young People (IAYP) — Gold, Silver, and Bronze.

Beyond code, I'm an active member of the IEEE Student Chapter, the Community Outreach Club, and the Competitive & Higher Education Club at SIT. I believe the best engineers are not just great coders — they're empathetic, communicative, and purpose-driven.
